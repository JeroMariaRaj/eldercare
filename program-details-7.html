Art & Music Therapy

Program Overview

Creative expression has profound benefits for the aging brain. Our Art & Music Therapy sessions allow seniors to explore painting, crafting, singing, and instrument playing in a supportive, judgment-free environment.

Benefits

  • Improves fine motor skills.
  • Elevates mood and reduces stress.
  • Provides a non-verbal emotional outlet.
  • Unlocks positive memories through music.

Included Services

  • Guided watercolor and acrylic painting.
  • Group sing-alongs and choir.
  • Pottery and clay sculpting.
  • Live musical performances.

Schedule

  • 10:00 AM Morning Painting
  • 11:30 AM Art Appreciation Discussion
  • 02:00 PM Group Sing-along
  • 03:30 PM Crafting & Scrapbooking
