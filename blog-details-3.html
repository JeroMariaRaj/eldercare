How Gentle Exercise Supports Senior Wellness

David Smith, PT

Oct 05, 2026

Physical activity is essential for maintaining mobility and strength. However, the type of exercise matters significantly as we age. Gentle, low-impact exercise can offer the same benefits as vigorous workouts, without the associated risks.

Walking and Stretching

Daily walking is one of the most accessible and effective forms of exercise. Paired with daily stretching, seniors can maintain flexibility and reduce stiffness.

Balance and Physiotherapy

Balance exercises and light strength exercises under the guidance of a physiotherapist help prevent falls, which is a leading cause of injury among older adults. Exercise safety should always be the priority.
